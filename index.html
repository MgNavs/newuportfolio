Reflection

“New University has given me the space to push my artistic capabilities to new perspectives.”

My experience with New University as a graphic design intern has pushed me to grow a lot as an artist. My goal as an intern was to push myself out of my artistic comfort zone. New University has given me the space to push my artistic capabilities to new perspectives. Going into this, I knew that I was more comfortable with graphic design in terms of manipulating images and text to make graphics, but I have always felt uneasy about drawing digital art. Now I feel more comfortable with digital art. Through this, I was able to experiment with different art styles based on the context of the article, which helped me learn how to effectively visually capture the article’s content. It also taught me the importance of time management and in meeting deadlines.
